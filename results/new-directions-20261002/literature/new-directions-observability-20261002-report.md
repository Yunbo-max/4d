# Observability branch: a question about recycled evidence
Date/cutoff: 2026-10-02. Status: exploratory parent problem, **not a method proposal, Natural Gate 0 PASS, or novelty PASS**. Six search queries; four primary works read at D2; no code, GPU, SSH, or experimental results produced.

## Proposed question
**When a 4D pipeline uses its own generated views as reconstruction evidence, can it distinguish a useful generative prior from additional independent observations—and does that distinction change which reconstructed motions or edits should be trusted?**

The consequential outcome is an accepted dynamic asset or camera/object edit that disagrees with a real held-out observation. The question is not whether synthetic views are intrinsically harmful. They can improve geometry and motion by exposing useful learned priors. The untested failure is that apparent geometric support or confidence may improve faster than held-out task accuracy because correlated generated evidence is counted repeatedly.

This is a new problem direction relative to our rejected surface correction, decoder reversal, and disagreement ranking. It does not rescue those methods. The current eight-asset census establishes neither prevalence nor this mechanism: it contains one backbone and does not log the required evidence lineage.

## Six-field parent problem sketch
- **Natural failure:** Hypothesized evidence inflation in deployed generator → multi-view reconstruction → 4D guidance pipelines: generated views agree with one another or a reconstructed proxy, yet hidden geometry/motion remains wrong and receives stronger acceptance. No natural rate or direct calibration failure has yet been demonstrated. Primary papers establish that this pipeline structure is real and that upstream errors can propagate; they do not establish this hypothesis.
- **Population:** Existing-video reconstruction/editing systems that combine actual source frames with generated novel views, rendered proxy views, or recursively completed caches. Include successful episodes and failures. Exclude unconstrained text-to-4D creation where recovering one actual hidden world is not the user task.
- **Prevalence:** Unknown. First acquire a fixed, unfiltered denominator of 12–20 naturally occluded real or benchmark episodes from at least two qualifying backends, with source/generation provenance and independently captured or rendered held-out views. First-six inspection is feasibility/development only. Synthetic duplicate controls cannot establish natural prevalence.
- **Consequence:** A confidence-based selection or edit-acceptance decision accepts an incorrect disoccluded silhouette, material motion, or occlusion ordering after camera redirection. Evaluate actual held-out error and accepted-failure rate at a fixed acceptance budget; source-view quality alone is insufficient. Do not infer contact correctness from a visually plausible view.
- **Strongest simple alternative:** Keep provenance and count all descendants of one source observation as one correlated evidence group; compare a simple fixed synthetic-evidence cap, source-only reconstruction, equal-compute random candidate selection, and ordinary coverage/visibility heuristics. If these solve the effect, no sophisticated steering method is justified. A generative prior can remain useful under this alternative.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** At matched compute, source fit, pose coverage, and output count, adding highly dependent generated views sometimes increases a native support/acceptance score without a matching reduction in held-out error. Adding an actual independent observation should improve the relationship more reliably. The key result is an interaction between evidence origin/dependence and score–error behavior, not merely “more views help” or “synthetic views hurt.”

## Closest primary work and what was actually read
1. **FreeOrbit4D**, SIGGRAPH 2026, [v2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.18993v2), §3.1–3.3, §4, §5, §6; [official repository](https://github.com/VVeiCao/FreeOrbit4D), Installation and Run on Your Own Video. D2.
   Its canonical completion combines the observed foreground view with four SV4D2-generated views in VGGT, then aligns the geometry into the scene and guides a video generator. This is a concrete natural pipeline for the question. Its experiments report visual/semantic metrics and user study; §6 acknowledges upstream segmentation/synthesis error propagation and heavy mutual occlusion limitations. The README recommends at least 46 GB VRAM and says heavy occlusion is unsupported. Full reproduction is therefore **not qualified on our 22 GB card**. Do not characterize its demonstrated completion gains as a failure.

2. **USplat4D / Uncertainty Matters in Dynamic Gaussian Splatting for Monocular 4D Reconstruction**, [v1 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12768v1), §4.1 Eq.3/7, §4.2–4.3, §5.1, Appendix D.3–D.4. D2.
   It already estimates observation-strength uncertainty, makes it anisotropic, selects reliable graph anchors, and propagates motion to weakly observed Gaussians. This directly collides with generic uncertainty-weighted 4D correction. Appendix D.4 says a failed initial 4D prior can leave insufficient useful motion information. Its extreme-view real-data examples lack GT; synthetic views provide quantitative evaluation. It does not by itself prove that generated evidence is over-counted. H100 timing is reported, but a <=22 GB memory profile was not verified.

3. **PREX / Preserve, Reveal, Expand**, [v1 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20961v1), §1, §3–4, §5/Table 2, Appendices B/G/H. D2.
   It already separates evidence-supported, disoccluded, and out-of-view regions, uses confidence with a trained adapter around a frozen backbone, and provides region-aware editing metrics. A new visibility mask or preserve/reveal benchmark would be a substantial collision. Appendix H identifies upstream 4D/projection errors propagating to edits; this supports investigating evidence provenance, not the proposed failure's prevalence. Appendix G reports approximately 66 GB additional GPU memory in its full model setting. It is neither a demonstrated 22 GB baseline nor strictly training-free merely because the backbone is frozen.

4. **DreamScene4D**, [v2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.02280v2), §3.2, §4.3, Appendix A.2/A.4 and Figure 9. D2.
   It separates camera, world motion, and object deformation. Figure 9 documents naturally occluded inpainting failures yielding flicker, and depth errors causing incorrect scene composition. These are concrete consequential failures, although they do not isolate recycled evidence. The runtime example uses a 40 GB A100; this is a device specification, not a measured memory requirement. Its code path is not qualified within 22 GB here.

**Additional collisions/guards, not independently deep-read in this branch:** Parent supplied [task-specific Bayesian next-best-view selection](https://papers.avt.im/next-view-planning/) and [World from Motion](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/amri/projects/world-from-motion/) / arXiv:2607.01202. Parent reports the latter improves motion/view accuracy. This is a strong counterexample to “synthetic evidence is bad,” and must be prosecuted in the next review. [Taming Video Models for 3D and 4D Generation via Zero-Shot Camera Control](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Song_Taming_Video_Models_for_3D_and_4D_Generation_via_Zero-Shot_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf) is also an outstanding exact comparator. ## Cheapest decisive screen before inventing a mechanism
**First use cached real pipeline episodes, CPU only.** Required artifacts are actual source frames, generated views with their parent/seed lineage, camera poses, reconstruction outputs, any native support/confidence or acceptance score, and independently held-out views/geometry. Cached output availability across two backends is currently **unknown**. Existing ActionMesh arrays alone do not supply this evidence.

For six developmental episodes, retain every version of each generated-view/reconstruction stage. Check whether the pipeline actually exposes and uses a support/confidence score. Compare its ordering to held-out disoccluded geometry/motion error and to source-only fit. Stratify by observation-backed versus never-observed/temporarily-occluded surfaces using evaluation-only GT visibility; input-only visibility estimates are a separate deployability check. Fix alignment from actual source observations, never refit to held-out GT. Use material GT correspondences where available; our old frame0 nearest-neighbor motion readout cannot certify semantic motion.

Then, only if artifacts and a natural effect exist, freeze a small inference experiment:
- Source-only; source plus fixed generated views; source plus new independently generated groups; source plus one true held-out observation reallocated as input. Evaluate all on a separate untouched target set.
- Match view angle/count and compute where possible. Separate exact duplicates (numerical sanity control) from correlated natural generations and genuinely independent capture. Random additional views and maximum visibility/coverage selection are mandatory simple alternatives.
- Use seeds/pipelines as correlated repeats, with the scene as the independent unit. More frames or samples do not increase the asset denominator.
- Inspect both absolute task error and selective risk at a fixed retained fraction. A raw VGGT confidence or render-support scalar is **not** a calibrated posterior probability. Probability calibration/coverage claims require an actual probability contract and held-out scene-level calibration; do not manufacture one by naming a score “uncertainty.”
- Proposed developmental stopping rule: no further mechanism work if all six episodes show score changes tracking held-out gains, or if a source-provenance cap explains the entire observed effect. Six episodes can reject an attractive local story, not establish a population prevalence.

**Resource contract:** CPU cached-artifact audit uses no VRAM. A reconstruction-only submodule can be profiled serially with an 18 GiB process reservation, 2 GiB card headroom, <=300 seconds per episode, stopping on OOM; its actual memory is **unknown until measured**. This is a bounded feasibility test, not a promise that VGGT or a second 4D backend fits. Do not run full FreeOrbit4D/PREX as if they fit. Existing low-memory ActionMesh is usable for a later cross-representation comparison, but not as a substitute for the missing natural evidence-recycling pipeline.

## Kill / reroute conditions and scientific value
- **Kill the current hypothesis** if increased generated support consistently predicts genuine held-out improvement and no residual acceptance failure remains after controlling visibility, motion magnitude, and source fit.
- **Kill method development** if provenance grouping or a fixed evidence cap already resolves failures; retain a modest empirical diagnostic only if prevalence and consequence justify it.
- **Reroute the problem** if natural episodes lack consequential errors, usable confidence/selection behavior, independent held-out evidence, or a feasible second backend. Do not manufacture a benchmark from exact duplicated images and call that the natural failure.
- **No cross-model claim** from one ActionMesh run or six scene repeats. Scientific value would be a validated distinction between independent evidence, correlated generative priors, and downstream decision reliability across representations; that needs at least two real pipelines.
- The generic branches “confidence weighting for occlusion,” “region-aware editing preservation,” and “task-aware next view” are not retained as novelty candidates. The issue is evidence accounting at inference, conditional on an important natural failure—not a renamed uncertainty energy.

## Recommendation
Retain this **question** for a short artifact-availability and natural-failure census. Do not implement a new method yet. Its appeal is a potentially general inference failure that can be studied with frozen models; its biggest weaknesses are unmeasured prevalence, unqualified <=22 GB access to the most relevant backends, and an incomplete collision search around correlated Bayesian evidence/self-training. A better-supported branch should take priority if those obstacles do not clear quickly.
